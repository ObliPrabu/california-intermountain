## 🌤️ Climate

California has a **mild climate**, but the **Great Basin** near California had a very **hot climate with limited rainfall**.

California's temperatures switched from cold to hot often, which is why it is considered a mild climate.

| Area | Climate |
|------|---------|
| 🌴 California | Mild, with temperatures that switch between cold and hot |
| 🏜️ Great Basin | Very hot, with limited rainfall |
